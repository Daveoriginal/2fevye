WHO WE ARE

A trusted partner in digital capability and management excellence.

2fevye Consulting (BN2652548), headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, is a forward-thinking ICT and Management Consultancy firm. We are a Certiport Authorized Testing Centre (CATC) affiliated with Certiport Inc. (Utah, USA), the global leader in digital skills certification, including the Internet and Core Computing Certification® (IC3™)

Our vision is to build a structured roadmap for success — bridging the digital skills gap, enhancing human potential, and empowering individuals and organizations to achieve digital transformation and career goals.

We offer a complete certification and consultation solution for schools, universities, IT professionals, and enterprises. Our offerings include IC3®, Microsoft Office Specialist, Microsoft Technology Associate, Adobe Certified Associate, HP Accredited Technical Associate, Autodesk Certified User, Intuit QuickBooks, and coding tracks in C++, Java, and more.

As a Certiport Authorized Testing Centre through Three Digital Innovation Solution Ltd, affiliated with Certiport Inc. (Utah, USA), we support access to globally recognised digital skills certification pathways.

What is a Certiport Authorized Testing Centre?

A CATC is an authorised testing centre that provides access to eligible Certiport certification examinations and related programmes.

What is a Certiport exam?

A Certiport exam is a performance-based assessment that measures practical digital skills in areas such as Microsoft Office applications, IT fundamentals, coding and digital literacy. Candidates complete tasks in real or simulated environments, and successful candidates receive a globally recognised digital credential.

Who can enrol for your certification programmes?

Individuals, students, professionals, schools and organisations can enquire about available certification pathways.

Can you train multiple employees or students?

Yes. Organisations and institutions can contact 2FEVYE to discuss group training and programme requirements.

Are virtual training options available?

Training delivery arrangements should be confirmed with the company when making an enquiry.

How do we begin an engagement?

Contact the team with your training, consulting or institutional objectives so that the appropriate next steps can be discussed.

How do I register for an examination?

Contact the company to confirm the relevant certification, eligibility, schedule and registration procedure.
